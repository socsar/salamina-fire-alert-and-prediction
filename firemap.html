    About the Wildfire Smoke Layer (Beta)

    This layer visualizes surface concentrations of PM2.5 (fine particulate matter with a diameter of 2.5 micrometers or less) originating from wildfires. PM2.5 is a key air pollutant that can significantly impact health and visibility.

    Key Features:

    • Data Source: Regional Deterministic Air Quality Analysis (RDAQA) system, Environment and Climate Change Canada.
    • Update Frequency: Updated hourly, providing near real-time smoke information.
    • Resolution: Data is processed at approximately 1km spatial resolution using bicubic resampling for smoother visualization.
    • Coverage: Currently covers North America.
    • Status: This is a BETA layer. Accuracy and availability may vary.

    Coming Soon:

    • Integration of global smoke data sources.
    • Incorporation of PurpleAir sensor data for localized PM2.5 readings.
    • Animated 72-hour smoke forecast.

    This layer is intended for informational purposes to understand potential smoke impacts from wildfires. Always consult official air quality advisories from your local health and environmental agencies.

    About FireMap.live

    Firemap.live is a comprehensive global wildfire tracking platform that integrates satellite data and authoritative fire information from agencies across the world. It is powered by FireDB, which enhances the data from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) with authoritative fire points sourced from agencies in the U.S., Canada, Europe, and Australia. This combination allows Firemap.live to provide real-time, standardized global fire statuses.

    The platform delivers detailed insights through real-time fire perimeters, heat maps, and fire status updates. By combining early detection data from NASA FIRMS with local fire authorities, Firemap.live offers one of the most reliable tools for wildfire monitoring and response. It is particularly valuable for emergency responders, researchers, and anyone needing to stay updated on global fire events.

    Disclaimer: FireMap.live provides wildfire data for informational purposes only. Although we strive for accuracy, this data should not be relied upon for critical safety decisions. Always consult your local authorities or official emergency management agencies for the most accurate and timely information.
